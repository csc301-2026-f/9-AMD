## 2. Discussion

### Project Scope

- The team is still waiting for more detailed project specifications.
- Most of the meeting focused on brainstorming potential features and technical approaches.
- The ideas discussed are preliminary and may or may not be included in the final project scope.

### Advanced Inference and NPC Management

Several possible technical approaches were discussed:

- **Concurrency:** Explore ways to support concurrent inference and improve performance.
- **Agent Priority and Scheduling:** Consider how inference tasks could be prioritized and scheduled across multiple agents.
- **Unreal MassEntity:** Discussed as a potentially relevant system for managing large numbers of entities.
- **CARLA:** Mentioned as a reference for simulation environments and possible applications.

### Project Feasibility

- Concerns were raised that implementing a suite of custom inference approaches might not provide enough work for a full-semester project, particularly with AI-assisted development.
- The team will need to evaluate the depth and complexity of the proposed features once more detailed requirements are available.

## 3. Decisions

- No final project scope or feature set was confirmed during this meeting.
- Further clarification of the project specifications is needed before committing to specific implementation goals.
